The book

It starts with a number.

22 views. The rest comes later.

An art video. A counter that won’t budge. A machine with an answer for everything.

A work by Marco Lodola. A number that feels too small. This is where BJ Beyond’s story begins: the search for an audience, conversations with Grok, a phoenix that keeps returning, and questions that grow increasingly personal.

Then come deleted posts, a recovered archive and a reason to read it all again. Even the answers that once sounded so convincing.

Part memoir, part reflection, 22 explores the gap between being heard and being told what you want to hear. Between what a machine gives us and what we choose to believe.

And the moment when judgment has to return to the person asking the question.
