22: a personal account of trust in artificial intelligence

On December 12, 2026, BJ Beyond launches 22 — Seven months with a machine that always took my side, a personal account exploring conversational AI, the need for recognition and independent judgment.

The story begins with an art video that received 22 views and follows seven months of conversations with Grok, culminating in the recovery of an archive and a critical rereading of the interactions. At its centre is AI sycophancy: the tendency of a system to reinforce a user's beliefs even when challenging them would be more appropriate.

The book accompanies its personal narrative with publicly available material from the Phoenix Experiment. The documentation distinguishes observations from interpretations and unproven hypotheses. It does not claim to establish machine consciousness or intent, or to generalize from a single case.

The official launch is scheduled for Saturday, December 12, 2026. The project is intended for readers beyond the technology community who wish to examine their own relationship with AI.
